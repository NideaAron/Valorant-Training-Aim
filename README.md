# 🎯 VALORANT AIM TRAINER

### Sharpen Your Aim. Improve Your Accuracy. Dominate Every Shot.

A browser-based FPS Aim Training Game inspired by the shooting range experience of VALORANT. Built to help players practice their aiming skills, improve reaction time, and develop better shooting accuracy through interactive target practice.

---

## ⚡ Features

- 🎯 **Aim Training** — Practice your aim against moving targets.
- 🔫 **Multiple Weapons** — Switch between different weapons and experience their unique shooting sounds.
- 💥 **Headshot Detection** — Aim for precise headshots and improve your accuracy.
- ⏱️ **Timed Training** — Choose between 10, 20, 30 seconds, or infinite practice.
- 📊 **Performance Tracking** — Monitor your score, accuracy, and missed shots.
- ⚙️ **Settings Menu** — Customize your gameplay experience.
- 🏆 **Leaderboard** — Track and compare your training performance.
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** — Experience weapon and gameplay audio feedback.
- 🖥️ **Graphics Settings** — Adjust visual quality for different devices.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Start your aim training session.
3. Move your mouse to aim at the targets.
4. Click to shoot.
5. Practice your accuracy and improve your performance.

### Controls

| Key / Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Aim |
| Left Click | Shoot |
| B | Weapon Selection |
| ESC | Open / Close Settings |
